About Puja Darshan

Puja Darshan is a digital platform dedicated to documenting and sharing the beauty, culture and traditions of Durga Puja in Bangladesh.

Through photographs, videos and mandir-focused galleries, Puja Darshan aims to showcase festive decorations, cultural programs, devotional moments and the vibrant atmosphere of Durga Puja celebrations.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to create a simple digital space where people can discover and enjoy Durga Puja celebrations, mandir decorations and cultural moments from different locations in Bangladesh.

We want to preserve these visual memories and make them accessible to people who love Puja, culture, photography and Bengali heritage.

What You Can Explore

  • Durga Puja photo galleries
  • Durga Puja videos
  • Puja mandir and pandal decorations
  • Cultural programs and celebrations
  • Festive and devotional moments

Our Vision

Puja Darshan aims to grow into a dedicated digital archive of Durga Puja celebrations, helping preserve and share the cultural beauty and festive traditions of Bangladesh.
